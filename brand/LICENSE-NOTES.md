# Brand mark license notes

`mark.svg` is an original Corvin geometric mark: a continuous-stroke raven head
in profile with a gold square eye. It is not derived from third-party logos or
stock raven artwork.

## Use

Clients may place the mark left of the wordmark on light grounds, or above the
word on square app icons. Prefer IBM Plex Sans SemiBold for the `Corvin`
wordmark when rendering text beside the mark.
